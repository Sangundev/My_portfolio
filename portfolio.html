Decision principles

How I make design decisions.

Four questions that keep me honest, from the first sketch to the last review.

How do I start a project?

I ask who it’s for, what they should do, and what success looks like. Then I sketch before I open Figma.

How do I choose between two designs?

I compare both against the main task. The one that gets someone to the goal faster wins.

How do I work with developers?

I hand over a tidy Figma file with named components and short notes, and stay around for review.

What do I do when I’m stuck?

I step away, look at real examples, and change one thing at a time.
