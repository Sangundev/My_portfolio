Decision principles

How I make design decisions.

The following questions guide me when I start designing, supporting the implementation and practicality of a digital product in real business situations.

How do I make an accurate judgment when the client has not clearly defined the product they want?

I base my initial ideas on business goals and brand direction, then continuously adjust through user feedback, data analysis and real-world testing.

When design value conflicts with brand direction, how should I choose?

I prioritize listening to users, analyzing behavior and comparing it with business goals to identify what needs improving, rather than relying only on personal opinion.

How do I maintain a clear user experience when working with complex systems?

I use task breakdown, reveal information step by step and provide instant feedback, so users can easily approach the system and make decisions without being overloaded with information.

How do I turn design into business capability on a digital platform?

I start from the real business model, identify users’ common needs and problems, then build reusable design components that can scale as the business grows.
